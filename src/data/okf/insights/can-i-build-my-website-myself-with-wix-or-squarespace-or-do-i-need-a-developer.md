# Summary
Wix and Squarespace are genuinely the right call for many businesses, despite being direct competition at the smaller end of agency work. DIY works when the idea is still being validated and the business might look completely different in a year, when the site is simple enough that a template genuinely covers it, and when there is real time to invest, since DIY trades money for hours rather than being free. It stops being the right call when you need functionality the template and its plugin marketplace do not offer, when you expect to grow into something more complex within a year or two and would end up paying for two websites, when proprietary platform lock-in matters because migrating off usually means starting again, when differentiation matters competitively and template constraints produce a generic result, or when SEO and performance need to hold up across many pages or a real product catalog.
