# Summary
Concrete Australian budget ranges instead of the usual 'it depends'. Websites: brochure or marketing site $5K–$20K+ (the bottom of that range is template or AI-generated and looks generic, custom design starts at $20K), content-heavy site $20K+, e-commerce $20K+, web app or customer portal $30K+, complex or enterprise site $80K+. Apps: MVP or proof of concept $2K+, simple single-purpose app $10K+, custom business app across both platforms $30K+ (cross-platform has mostly closed the gap with native for standard functionality, native still wins on best-possible UX), e-commerce or marketplace app $70K+, complex or enterprise app $100K+. Ongoing costs matter too: hosting $0–$400+ a month depending on traffic, maintenance and support around 10% of build cost a year, a negligible domain fee, and content updates most teams handle themselves. These are ranges rather than quotes; the number that matters is built around specific features.
