# Summary
Most AI conversations start from zero, working only with what you paste in that moment rather than the depth of knowledge a business has accumulated. Pairing a structured note store (Obsidian) with a local model (Ollama) and a connector (Hermes) lets the AI answer from that store first, and keeps the data on your own machine, which matters when notes contain client or sensitive information. In practice this surfaced prior research during Pixel Assist's PII scrubbing work: the agent flagged a similar earlier engagement and offered its libraries, data flow requirements, and lessons, turning days of reconstruction into minutes. The setup is still fiddly, but it will get easier to package, so the question worth answering now is whether your knowledge is in good enough shape to be worth querying, which is what the Open Knowledge Format addresses.
