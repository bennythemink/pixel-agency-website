# Summary
Adopting AI because of competitor pressure or fear of being late is how time and money get wasted; the businesses that get real value started from a specific problem and worked backwards to the solution, which is sometimes AI and sometimes not. Eight problem patterns are a genuinely good fit: expertise that does not scale, answering the same questions repeatedly, manual processes that should not be manual, content production at volume, customers who cannot self-serve, slow staff onboarding, information lost across meetings and emails, and decisions made without surfaced data. If the problem does not match one of those patterns, AI is probably not the right tool yet, and knowing that is itself useful.
