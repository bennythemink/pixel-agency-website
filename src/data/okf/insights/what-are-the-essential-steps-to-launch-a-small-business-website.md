# Summary
Design, build, launch is only part of it; the businesses that get results do the things most people skip. Know what the site actually needs to achieve and how it differs from the competitors customers are already looking at, which is what a good brief is for. Lock in the domain and business name in week one rather than week ten, to avoid finding it taken or caught up in someone else's trademark. Do not wait on content before starting design, since copy is easier to write once there is a layout to react to. Choose the platform on need rather than trend — WordPress for real customisation, Shopify for something that runs itself — and let whoever is building it make the call. Most importantly, have a marketing plan before launch, not after: a good site with no traffic is indistinguishable from a bad one, and clients who convert well but lack volume make it obvious the site was never the problem. Few have a real SEO plan and almost none are thinking about how their business shows up in AI-generated answers.
