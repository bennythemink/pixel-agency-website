# Summary
Choosing an agency means trusting before you have proof, but the right questions surface most of the risk beforehand. Ask what they would do about a problem they have not solved before (a real process versus reaching for whatever they already know); what technology they are actually using and whether it matches the quote (one client was quoted native iOS and Android pricing for what turned out to be a cross-platform build); who will actually do the work rather than who is in the pitch; where the delivery team is based, which affects response times, overlap, and familiarity with Australian accessibility standards and payment providers; who owns the IP; whether anything carries a recurring fee and who receives it; whether they can show references of comparable size and complexity; and how quickly the quote arrived relative to how many real questions preceded it. No single question guarantees a good outcome, but answering them clearly and without defensiveness tells you something.
