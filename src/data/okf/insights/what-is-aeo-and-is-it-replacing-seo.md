# Summary
AEO is not replacing SEO, it is building on top of it, and optimising for one while ignoring the other means being overtaken. Answer Engine Optimization structures content so AI answer engines such as Google's AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search can pull it directly into a synthesised answer rather than merely linking to it. Traditional SEO operates at the page level through titles, keywords, backlinks, and ranking; AEO operates closer to the fact level through clear definitions, citable statistics, and sections structured to be lifted out cleanly and attributed back. This matters because a large and growing share of searches now end without a click. The practical takeaway is not to abandon SEO but to write content both a search engine and an AI system can use: clear structure, direct answers near the top, and facts stated plainly enough to quote.
