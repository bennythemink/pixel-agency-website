# Summary
A checklist of signals that a site needs work: poor or absent mobile optimisation, confusing or overly deep navigation, accessibility and readability problems such as low contrast or key details hidden behind extra clicks, a site that no longer matches the brand, obvious stock photography, out-of-date messaging, generic content that would suit any competitor, chasing an already-dated trend, hesitating before sending someone the link, competitors who plainly look better, and a high bounce rate or healthy traffic that produces no leads. Recognising several of these is worth taking seriously, but it does not automatically mean a full rebuild: sometimes a scoped fix resolves it, sometimes redevelopment is the honest answer, and a discovery phase is how to tell which.
