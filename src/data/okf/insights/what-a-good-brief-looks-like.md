# Summary
A good project brief doesn't need every answer, it needs to clearly define the problem. That means covering business context, the objective, the end user, why the project matters now, post-launch/traffic plans, reference examples, and an honest budget and timeline. The finer detail can and should surface later during discovery or scoping, the brief just needs to be clear enough for a team to start asking the right questions.
