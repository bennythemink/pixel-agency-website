# Summary
A rebuild is a significant investment and is not always the right response to a website problem. It is warranted when the tech stack is deprecated or unsupported and accumulating security and integration risk, when the code actively blocks business value, when the UX and data flow are structurally wrong rather than cosmetically dated, or when annual maintenance cost approaches the cost of rebuilding. It is probably not warranted when the design merely feels dated but the structure works, when a single integration is broken, or when low traffic and conversion have not actually been tested and may be an SEO or volume problem rather than a site problem. Making this call with evidence rather than instinct is what a discovery phase or a consulting conversation is for.
