# Summary
Switching agencies is normal and straightforward if the right things are in hand first. Secure domain registrar access under your own control (an agency owning the domain outright is a serious problem to resolve before anything else), full hosting admin rather than a limited FTP login, a full-permission CMS or platform account rather than a restricted editor login, the actual source repository for custom builds rather than what happens to be on the server, ownership of Analytics and Search Console so years of historical data are not lost, a documented DNS export before anything changes since DNS mistakes are a common cause of sites and business email going down, and third-party accounts and API keys for payment, email marketing, and CRM integrations. Keep a short overlap period and only let the old agency's access lapse once the new arrangement is confirmed working. A good agency will hand all of this over without pushback, and pushback is itself informative.
